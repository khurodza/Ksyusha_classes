Practice for Lesson 01 (Unit 2A "To the Rescue!"): past simple vs. past continuous, and past simple verbs (regular and irregular).

Task 1: The cat in the tree

Complete the story with the past simple or past continuous form of the verbs in brackets. The first one is done for you.
Last Saturday my brother and I were playing (play) football in the park when we (hear) a strange noise. A small cat (sit) at the top of a tall tree and it (cry) loudly. While my brother (try) to climb the tree, an old man (stop) and (call) the fire station. It (rain) when the firefighters (arrive), but they (rescue) the cat in five minutes. When I (get) home, I (tell) my mum the whole story.
Now choose when or while.
1. ... we were walking home, we saw a fox.
2. The dog was sleeping ... the phone rang.
3. ... the bird flew away, the cat jumped.

Task 3: Mia and Sam chat about the weekend

Sam

Mia
(you / enjoy) it?

Mia

We (arrive) home at 10 pm and I (feel) really tired.
Mia
And (your brother / play) football on Sunday?
