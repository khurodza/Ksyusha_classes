Homework 02 — Guess What Happened?

Practice for Lesson 02 ("Guess What Happened?"): telling a story with the past simple and past continuous.

Task 1: When or while?

Complete the sentences with the past simple or past continuous form of the verbs in brackets.
Remember!
while + past continuous → the longer action: While I was walking …
when + past simple → the short action: … when it started to rain.

1. I (watch) TV when my mum (come) home.

2. While we (have) dinner, the phone (ring).

3. When I (see) Tom, he (wait) for the bus.

4. It (rain) when we (leave) school.

5. While my sister (read) a book, the cat (jump) on the table.

6. They (play) football when it (start) to snow.

7. I (drop) my phone while I (walk) to school.

8. When the teacher (open) the door, the students (talk).

9. While Dad (cook), he (burn) his finger.

10. My friends (not / listen) when I (tell) the joke.

Task 2: While or when?

Complete the sentences with while or when. Look at the verbs to help you.

1. I was brushing my teeth, the lights went out.

2. I was sleeping the alarm rang.

3. she was shopping, she met her teacher.

4. We were eating pizza Grandma arrived.

5. He hurt his leg he was running in the park.

6. I got to the station, the train was leaving.

7. The baby woke up we were watching a film.

8. My pen broke I was doing my homework.
